A remote partner for Lagos

Lagos moves fast. Your replies should too.

In Lagos, a customer who waits an hour for a reply has often bought somewhere else. Many businesses still rely on one or two people answering WhatsApp, checking DMs and copying orders into a notebook or spreadsheet. That works until it doesn’t: at weekends, after hours, or the day the order volume doubles.

AI automation lets software handle the predictable part of that work: answering common questions from your own price list, taking booking details, qualifying a lead, confirming an order and updating your records, while your staff handle negotiation, complaints and anything unusual.

BuildWise Group is a remote-first technology company. We serve Lagos businesses remotely and do not have a physical office, local walk-in service or field staff in Lagos. For most automation projects that is an advantage: no one sits in traffic for a meeting, and everything we agree is written down.

How an engagement works

Remote by design.
Clear at every step.

Every stage happens over video calls, WhatsApp and shared documents, so decisions are written down and nobody loses an afternoon to Third Mainland Bridge.

  1. 01

    Discovery call

    A short conversation about the work as it happens today: who does it, how often, which tools are involved and what a better outcome looks like.

  2. 02

    Map and prioritise

    We map the workflow, mark the steps worth automating and the ones that should stay human, and agree what “working” means in measurable terms.

  3. 03

    Build a focused first version

    One workflow, built around your existing tools, with logging, error handling and a clear way for a person to step in.

  4. 04

    Test with real cases

    We run it against real (or realistic) examples, including the awkward ones, and fix what breaks before anyone depends on it.

  5. 05

    Launch, measure, extend

    It goes live with your team, we watch the results together, and the evidence decides what we automate next.

Frequently asked questions

Questions, answered plainly.

Is BuildWise based in Lagos?

BuildWise Group is a remote-first company and does not have a physical office in Lagos. We serve Lagos businesses remotely, and all discovery, delivery and support happens by video call, WhatsApp and shared documents.

Can we meet in person?

Our standard way of working is remote. If an in-person session would genuinely help your project, raise it on the discovery call and we’ll tell you honestly whether we can arrange it.

How quickly can a first version go live?

It depends on the workflow and how quickly accounts such as WhatsApp Business Platform access can be set up. We give you a plan with stages after discovery rather than a promise before we understand the work.

Will the assistant understand Pidgin or Yoruba?

Modern AI models handle Nigerian English and Pidgin reasonably well, and Yoruba, Igbo and Hausa less consistently. We test with real messages from your customers before launch, and anything the assistant isn’t sure about goes to a person.

What if the AI gives a wrong answer?

Assistants answer from information you approve, such as prices and policies, and are told to hand over when they are unsure. Conversations are logged so you can review them, and we tune the system during the first weeks.

How is pricing handled?

We quote per project after discovery, in naira or US dollars. Ongoing costs, such as WhatsApp message charges and AI model usage, are shown separately.
