What AI automation means

Software that handles the routine, so people handle the judgement.

AI automation is the use of software, including AI models that can read and write language, to complete business tasks that used to need a person at every step. Classic automation follows fixed rules: when a form is submitted, copy it to a spreadsheet. AI automation can also deal with messy input, such as a customer’s WhatsApp message, a scanned invoice or an email asking three questions at once.

For a Nigerian business, that usually means answering the same customer questions at any hour, qualifying leads before a salesperson calls, booking appointments, moving data between systems without retyping it, and producing routine documents and reports.

BuildWise Group is a remote-first technology company. We work with businesses in Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt and across Nigeria, and with international clients, without a physical office. Discovery, design, delivery and support all happen remotely.

How an engagement works

Small first version.
Evidence before scale.

We would rather automate one workflow that works every day than ten that look good in a demo. Each stage ends with something you can see and decide on.

  1. 01

    Discovery call

    A short conversation about the work as it happens today: who does it, how often, which tools are involved and what a better outcome looks like.

  2. 02

    Map and prioritise

    We map the workflow, mark the steps worth automating and the ones that should stay human, and agree what “working” means in measurable terms.

  3. 03

    Build a focused first version

    One workflow, built around your existing tools, with logging, error handling and a clear way for a person to step in.

  4. 04

    Test with real cases

    We run it against real (or realistic) examples, including the awkward ones, and fix what breaks before anyone depends on it.

  5. 05

    Launch, measure, extend

    It goes live with your team, we watch the results together, and the evidence decides what we automate next.

Frequently asked questions

Questions, answered plainly.

How much does AI automation cost in Nigeria?

It depends on the workflow, the number of systems involved and the volume of messages or tasks. We quote after the discovery call and audit, in naira or US dollars. Running costs, such as WhatsApp message charges from Meta and AI model usage, are shown separately so you can see what you pay each month.

Do you have an office in Lagos or Abuja?

No. BuildWise Group is remote-first and does not have a physical office. We work with clients across Nigeria and internationally by video call, WhatsApp and shared documents, and that is how discovery, delivery and support happen.

What is an AI automation audit?

A short, structured review of where time goes in your business. After one call (and any examples you can share), we send a written view of the two or three workflows most worth automating, what each would involve, the risks, and a suggested first version. If automation isn’t worth it yet, we say so.

Will AI replace my staff?

That is not how we design systems. Automation takes over repetitive steps, such as answering the same question or retyping data, so your team can spend time on customers and decisions. Every system we build has a clear way for a person to step in.

Is customer data safe, and what about the NDPA?

We design with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 in mind: collect only what the workflow needs, tell customers how their data is used, control who can access it and choose providers carefully. We can’t give legal advice, so we recommend you confirm your obligations with a data protection professional.

Which tools and platforms do you work with?

Whatever your business already uses where it offers a way to connect, such as an API: CRMs, spreadsheets, email, booking tools, accounting software and the WhatsApp Business Platform. We use low-code automation tools where they are enough and custom code where they aren’t.

Do you work with businesses outside Nigeria?

Yes. Nigeria is our first market, and we work remotely with international clients too.
