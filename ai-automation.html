What we solve

Busy front desks lose
customers quietly.

None of these are failures of effort. They happen when the same few people answer messages, run the diary and serve the customer in front of them.

  1. 01

    Enquiries wait for a reply

    A customer asks about a treatment at 9pm or during a busy clinic. By the time someone replies, they have booked elsewhere.

  2. 02

    Booking happens by hand

    Staff copy names, numbers and times between WhatsApp, a notebook and the diary, then call to confirm, reschedule and chase.

  3. 03

    Follow-ups slip

    People who asked about prices, or did not come back after a consultation, are never contacted again because there is no time.

  4. 04

    The same questions, all day

    Prices, opening hours, preparation, aftercare and location: answered again and again, sometimes inconsistently.

  5. 05

    Nobody can see the pipeline

    Owners cannot tell how many enquiries came in, where they came from, or which ones turned into appointments.

How AI employees work

A clear job, approved
knowledge, limited powers.

An AI employee is not a chatbot left to improvise. It has a written role, works only from information you approve, can take only the actions its job allows, and hands over to a person when it should.

  1. 01

    A customer gets in touch

    On your website, the AI employee greets them, answers in plain language and asks what they need.

  2. 02

    It answers from your information

    Services, prices, durations, opening hours and policies come from what you approved, not from guesswork. If it doesn’t know, it says so.

  3. 03

    It takes a permitted action

    Take their details, check free times, make or request a booking, reschedule or cancel. Every action is checked against the business’s settings before it runs.

  4. 04

    It hands over when it should

    Medical questions, complaints, unusual requests and anything sensitive go to your team, with the customer’s details attached.

  5. 05

    Your team sees everything

    Enquiries and bookings appear on your owner screens, so nothing depends on someone remembering a message.

  • It never pretends a booking is confirmed. It says “booked” only when the booking system confirms, with a reference. Otherwise it says the request is received and your team will confirm.
  • It reads details back first. Before booking, it repeats the service, date, time and contact details and waits for the customer’s yes.
  • It says it is an AI. Customers are told they are talking to an AI assistant, and can reach a person.
  • It never takes payments. Staff does not handle card details or payments, by design.

Frequently asked questions

Before you book.

What exactly is an AI employee?

Software that does one front-desk job, such as answering enquiries and taking bookings, under rules you approve. It has a written role, a fixed set of actions it may take and a handover to your team. It does not make decisions that need a person.

Will it replace my receptionist?

No. It takes the repetitive first contact off your team’s hands, especially outside opening hours, so they can spend time with the customers in front of them. Anything sensitive or unusual still goes to a person.

Will it tell a customer they are booked when they are not?

No. It says “booked” only when the booking system confirms the appointment with a reference. Otherwise it tells the customer the request has been received and your team will confirm.

Does it work with the booking software we already use?

Sometimes. If your software has an API or official integration, we can scope a connection, quoted separately. If not, you can use Staff’s built-in diary, or keep your system and have the AI employee pass booking requests to your team.

Can it answer phone calls or WhatsApp messages?

The standard implementation covers your website. Customers can be handed to your team’s WhatsApp at any time. AI voice, inbound phone calls and automated WhatsApp messages are early-access or integration work: we confirm in the assessment whether they are ready for your business and what they would cost.

What do I get from the $250 assessment, and do I have to go ahead?

A working session, a review of your current process and tools, and a written plan with a fixed implementation quote. You are under no obligation to proceed.

Why are Staff and Buildwise charged separately?

They are different things. Staff is the software subscription that runs your AI employee. Buildwise AI Operations is our team looking after it every month. Keeping them separate shows you exactly what you pay for.

What happens if we use more than 200 minutes?

Nothing runs up by surprise. Usage limits stop calls at the limit. Extra minutes run only if you approve them in writing, at the rate in your order form. Written chat, enquiries and bookings are not counted as minutes.

Can it give medical or treatment advice?

No. It answers practical questions from information you approve and passes medical, clinical and treatment-suitability questions to your practitioners.

How is customer data handled?

Only what the job needs is collected. Owner screens are private and never indexed. Action logs record what was done, not what the customer said. Retention periods are agreed with you. See the security section of our proposal, available on request.
