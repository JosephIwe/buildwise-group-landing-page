What an AI employee is

A role, not a chatbot.

“AI employee” is a useful way to think about AI in a business, as long as it is taken literally. A human employee has a job description, access to the systems the job needs, a manager, and a record of the work they did. An AI employee, as we build it, has the same four things: a written role, limited access to your tools, a person who supervises it, and a log of every conversation and action.

Under the hood an AI employee is one or more AI agents plus the workflow automation around them: the integrations that read and update your CRM, calendar, spreadsheet or inbox, and the rules that decide when a person must take over. The “employee” framing matters because it forces the right questions before anything is built: what exactly is this role responsible for, what is it never allowed to do, and who checks its work?

It is not a replacement for your team. The work an AI employee does well is high-volume, repetitive and well defined. Judgement, relationships and anything with real risk stay with people, and a good AI employee hands those over quickly, with the context attached.

How we build AI employees

Engineered like a system.
Managed like a hire.

The parts that make an AI employee safe to rely on are designed before the first conversation, not added after the demo.

A

Role and knowledge

A clear brief and the right information.

  • A written role: duties, limits and escalation rules
  • Answers grounded in information you approve
  • Model choice matched to the task, cost and data location
  • Test cases built from real requests, including awkward ones

B

Tools and integrations

Access to exactly what the role needs.

  • Connections to your CRM, calendar, inbox or sheets through their APIs
  • Least-privilege access for every system
  • Confirmation steps for sensitive actions
  • Defences against prompt injection from customer messages and documents

C

Supervision and improvement

Your team stays in charge.

  • Handover to a person with the full conversation
  • A log of every conversation and action
  • Regular review of mistakes and edge cases
  • Changes tested before they go live

How an engagement works

One role.
Evidence before scale.

An AI employee earns more responsibility the same way a new hire does: by doing a narrow job reliably first.

  1. 01

    Discovery call

    We look at where your team’s time goes and pick the one role where an AI employee would remove the most repetitive work with the least risk.

  2. 02

    Write the job description

    Duties, tone of voice, the systems it may read or update, what it must never do, and exactly when it hands over to a person.

  3. 03

    Build and connect

    We build the agent and the integrations around it, with logging and a simple way for your team to review its work.

  4. 04

    Supervised trial

    It runs on real (or realistic) cases with a person checking its work, and we fix what goes wrong before anyone depends on it.

  5. 05

    Go live and extend

    Once the evidence says it is reliable, it takes on its role fully, and we decide together whether to widen its duties or add a second role.

Built on our own work

Staff: our AI employee platform, in validation.

Staff is the applied-AI employee platform we are building and validating on everyday work such as enquiries and bookings. Its example employee, Maya, is an AI receptionist, and we have worked with an aesthetics business on Maya. Staff is not yet generally available, so for clients we build AI employees as projects, using what we learn from Staff.

We also build and run Huntly, a live AI job-search assistant, so the agent, integration and evaluation patterns we use for clients are ones we depend on ourselves.

An AI employee can be wrong. Any system built on a language model can misread a request. We design for that with narrow permissions, confirmation steps, logs and fast handover to people, and we will recommend a simpler automation, or no AI at all, where a mistake would be costly.

Frequently asked questions

Questions, answered plainly.

What is an AI employee?

An AI agent given one defined role in a business, such as front desk or lead follow-up, with access to the tools that role needs, written limits, and a person who supervises its work. It is software, not a person, and it should always say it is an AI assistant.

How is an AI employee different from a chatbot?

A scripted chatbot follows a fixed decision tree and usually only talks. An AI employee understands free-form requests and can take actions you allow, such as booking an appointment or updating your CRM, and it hands work to people when it should.

Will an AI employee replace my staff?

That is not how we design them. They take on the repetitive, high-volume part of a role so your team can spend time on customers, judgement and the work that needs a person.

Can I buy Staff or Maya today?

Not yet. Staff is in validation. We build AI employees for clients as projects in the meantime, and we will say so plainly when Staff is available.

Which tools can an AI employee work with?

Usually anything with an API or another reliable integration: WhatsApp Business Platform, website chat, email, calendars, CRMs, spreadsheets and many booking systems. We confirm each connection in discovery and tell you if one cannot be done safely.

How much does an AI employee cost?

We quote after discovery. Build cost depends on the role and the integrations; running costs, such as AI model usage and WhatsApp message charges, are shown separately so you can see them.
