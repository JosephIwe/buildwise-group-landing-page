What AI visibility actually is

There is no secret file. There is a lot of careful work.

AI search visibility (sometimes called generative engine optimisation, or GEO) is the work of making a business easy for AI-powered search to find, understand and recommend. Systems such as ChatGPT search, Perplexity, Gemini and Google’s AI Overviews mostly build answers from pages they can crawl and index, then cite the sources they rely on.

Google says there are no special requirements to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode beyond normal SEO best practice: pages must be crawlable and indexable, easy to reach through internal links, and useful. OpenAI says sites that allow its OAI-SearchBot crawler can appear in ChatGPT search answers, and that this is separate from its GPTBot training crawler.

So the work is real but unglamorous: make sure crawlers aren’t blocked (including by your CDN or firewall), make every page say clearly who you are, what you do and for whom, publish content that genuinely answers your customers’ questions, mark up facts with accurate structured data, and build a presence on trustworthy third-party sites. No one can guarantee an AI system will recommend you, and anyone who does is guessing.

Frequently asked questions

Questions, answered plainly.

Can you get my business recommended by ChatGPT?

No one can guarantee that. What we can do is remove the obstacles (blocked crawlers, unclear pages, inconsistent information) and build the signals AI search systems rely on: clear, useful pages, accurate structured data and genuine presence on trusted third-party sites.

Is AI visibility different from SEO?

Mostly it is good SEO with more attention to clarity and to how your business is described across the web. Google says normal SEO best practice applies to its AI features. The differences are in details, such as allowing AI search crawlers and measuring visits from AI assistants.

Do I need an llms.txt file?

It can’t hurt, but no major AI search provider has said it uses llms.txt to rank or cite sites. It is not a substitute for crawlable, clear pages.

Should I block AI crawlers?

That is a business decision. Blocking search crawlers such as OAI-SearchBot removes you from those AI search answers. Blocking training crawlers such as GPTBot or Google-Extended is a separate choice that doesn’t, according to the providers, affect search. We explain the trade-offs.

How long until we see results?

Technical fixes can take effect within days of being crawled. Content and authority take months. We report on the data honestly every month.

How do you measure AI visibility?

Search Console impressions and clicks, analytics referrals from AI assistants such as chatgpt.com and perplexity.ai where they pass a referrer, enquiries that mention AI tools, and periodic manual checks of how AI assistants describe you.
