The problems we address

Security gaps that
slow good products down.

Most weaknesses aren’t exotic. They sit in everyday decisions about access, data and configuration, and they are easiest to fix once someone has mapped them clearly.

  1. 01

    Exposed application data

    Records, files or personal data reachable by people who shouldn’t see them, through the interface, an API response or a misplaced storage setting.

  2. 02

    Weak authentication or authorisation

    Sign-in, session and permission checks that can be bypassed, or that let one customer act on another customer’s data.

  3. 03

    Risky cloud and deployment configuration

    Over-broad permissions, publicly exposed services, secrets in code or pipelines, and defaults nobody revisited after launch.

  4. 04

    Security gaps in AI-enabled products

    Assistants and agents that can be steered by untrusted input, leak context they were given, or hold more tool access than their job needs.

  5. 05

    Unclear remediation priorities

    A long list of issues and no clear view of which ones carry real business risk, so the important fixes wait behind the easy ones.

Service capabilities

Three areas,
one connected view.

Each engagement covers the capabilities, systems and test types agreed in its scope. Not every review includes every check below; we recommend what fits your system and risk.

A

Web application & API security

How your product decides who someone is, what they can do and what data they get back.

  • Authentication, session handling and authorisation controls
  • Common application weaknesses, such as those in the OWASP Top 10
  • API access control and sensitive-data exposure in responses
  • Input validation and security-relevant configuration

B

Cloud & deployment security

The infrastructure, permissions and pipelines your application depends on.

  • Cloud configuration and access permissions, within the agreed accounts and services
  • Exposed services and risky deployment settings
  • How secrets and keys are stored, shared and rotated
  • Security controls in CI/CD and application infrastructure

C

AI application security

LLM-powered features, AI agents and third-party AI integrations, reviewed as part of the whole system.

  • Prompt injection through user input, documents and retrieved content
  • Sensitive-data exposure through prompts, context and outputs
  • Access control in retrieval, memory and connected data
  • Unsafe tool permissions and agent actions, with practical mitigations for your design

Our assessment process

Scoped first.
Evidence throughout.

Active testing only starts once the system owner has authorised it in writing and we have agreed exactly what is in scope, when, and what is excluded.

  1. 01

    Discovery & scope

    We learn how the system works, what matters most to the business and where the risk is likely to sit, then agree targets, environments, test types, timing and exclusions in writing.

  2. 02

    Authorised assessment

    We review and test only what the written scope and authorisation cover, using the access you provide, with a named contact on your side throughout.

  3. 03

    Evidence-based findings

    Each finding is checked by hand and documented with what we observed, how it can be reproduced and what it could mean in practice.

  4. 04

    Prioritised remediation

    Findings are ranked by severity and business impact, with clear, practical fixes your engineers can plan around.

  5. 05

    Remediation & verification Optional

    If it is part of the agreed scope, we help implement fixes and then verify that they resolve the original findings.

Deliverables

A report written for the boardroom and the codebase.

Leaders get a clear picture of risk. Engineers get the detail to reproduce and fix each issue. Everyone gets the same order of priorities.

  1. 1

    Executive summary

    Overall posture, the most important risks and recommended next steps, in plain language.

  2. 2

    Technical findings & evidence

    What was found, where, how to reproduce it, and the supporting evidence.

  3. 3

    Severity & business impact

    Why each finding matters: who could exploit it, what it could expose and how likely that is.

  4. 4

    Prioritised remediation plan

    Fixes in order of risk reduction, with guidance specific to your stack.

  5. 5

    Retest or verification

    Confirmation that fixes work, where retesting is included in the agreed engagement.

Frequently asked questions

Before you enquire.

What does the review cover?

That depends on your system and what you need. During discovery we agree the applications, APIs, cloud accounts and AI features in scope, the environments we may use, the types of testing, timing and anything excluded. We confirm all of this in writing before any testing starts.

Do you need authorisation before testing?

Yes. Active testing needs written authorisation from the owner of each system in scope, and must follow the rules of any cloud or hosting provider involved. If a system belongs to someone else, such as a client or supplier, they need to authorise it too.

What will we receive?

A written report with an executive summary, technical findings and supporting evidence, severity and business-impact explanations, and a prioritised remediation plan. If retesting is part of the engagement, you also receive confirmation of which findings are resolved.

Can you help fix what you find?

Yes, if you want us to. BuildWise designs and builds software, so remediation support can be added to the scope: from advising your engineers to implementing agreed fixes, followed by verification.

How is sensitive information handled?

We ask only for the access the scope needs, and prefer dedicated test accounts and non-production environments where that is practical. How credentials, data and findings are shared, stored and removed is agreed in the engagement terms. Please don’t send passwords, keys or details of unfixed vulnerabilities through the enquiry form or WhatsApp.

Is this a penetration test or a compliance certification?

It is a scoped security assessment with remediation support. It does not certify compliance with any standard, and no review can guarantee a system is free of vulnerabilities. If a customer, insurer or framework requires a specific type of test, tell us at the start and we’ll say plainly whether this review fits.

How is it priced, and do you work internationally?

Pricing is set by scope, so we quote once we understand the system and what you need reviewed. We work remotely with businesses in any country.
