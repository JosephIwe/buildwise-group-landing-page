Frequently asked questions

Questions, answered plainly.

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

What does Buildwise Group do?

Buildwise Group is an independent, remote-first technology company working with businesses in Nigeria and internationally. We design, engineer and deploy AI automation, AI agents, WhatsApp automation, business process automation, custom software and websites. We also build and run our own products: Huntly, Traider, Staff and AskNex (opens in a new tab). Security reviews for applications and cloud setups are a supporting service.

What types of AI products do you build?

Products where AI does real work inside a workflow: assistants that evaluate and prepare information, as Huntly does for job seekers; AI employees that handle conversations such as enquiries and bookings, as Staff is designed to; and research tools that structure data, as in Traider. For client work we start from the problem and use AI only where it genuinely helps.

Can Buildwise automate existing business processes?

Yes. We look at how the work happens today, find repetitive steps such as copying data between systems, routing enquiries or preparing documents, and automate them around the tools you already use, keeping a person in the loop where judgement matters. The free AI Business Opportunity Scanner is a quick way to see where to start.

Does Buildwise build custom software?

Yes: web applications, SaaS products, internal tools, websites and integrations that connect the systems and data a team already relies on. We can also take over and improve an existing product.

Can you build an AI employee for my business?

It depends on the work. Staff is our AI employee platform, and Maya, an AI receptionist, is one example employee. Staff is still being validated on workflows such as enquiries and bookings, so we start by checking whether your workflow is a good fit and agreeing exactly what the AI employee should and shouldn’t handle, and when it hands over to a person.

What industries do you work with?

Our experience covers healthcare and aesthetics, recruitment and career technology, financial technology, business operations and digital products. Industry experience shows which of these come from client work and which from our own products. We’re happy to talk about other sectors.

How does a typical project begin?

With a conversation about the problem rather than the technology. We learn how the work happens today, what a better outcome looks like and what constraints apply, then propose a focused first version with clear acceptance criteria. Scope, timing and cost are agreed in writing before work starts.

How do I request a consultation or proposal?

Send a project enquiry or message us on WhatsApp (opens in a new tab). Tell us what you’re building or trying to change, who it’s for and any deadline. We’ll review it and reply; if the work looks like a fit, the next step is a call and then a written proposal.

Do you provide ongoing maintenance and support?

Yes, it can be part of an engagement, from website upkeep to updates and fixes for software we’ve built. What’s included is agreed for each project.
