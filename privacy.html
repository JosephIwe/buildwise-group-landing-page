Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated 23 October 2026

This page explains what information BuildWise Group receives through this website, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Project enquiries

You can send a project enquiry from our homepage or from our Application & Cloud Security Review page; both use the same form. When you send one, we receive your name, email address, project type and project description, plus anything optional you add: your company or organisation, budget range, preferred contact method and WhatsApp number. The form also notes which page of our website it was sent from.

We use this only to review your enquiry, reply to you and discuss a possible project. We do not add you to a mailing list or sell or share your details for marketing.

Enquiries are processed by Web3Forms, the form service that receives each submission and emails it to us. Web3Forms handles submissions under its own privacy policy (opens in a new tab), which sets how long it keeps them. The copy we receive stays in our email account until we delete it.

Security review enquiries and engagements

A security review enquiry is handled like any other project enquiry. Please do not include passwords, keys, other credentials or details of unfixed vulnerabilities in the form or on WhatsApp. If we go on to work together, the access, data and findings shared during the engagement, and how they are stored and removed, are covered by the written terms we agree for that engagement, not by this website policy.

WhatsApp

If you message us on WhatsApp, the conversation is handled by WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy policy. We use what you send only to reply and discuss your project.

AI Business Opportunity Scanner

Your answers and notes are processed in your browser and are not sent to us. A draft is kept in that browser tab so a refresh does not lose your progress, and it is cleared when you close the tab or start a new scan. If you choose “Request help”, WhatsApp opens with a summary you can edit, and nothing is sent unless you send it.

Website & AI Visibility Audit

The website address you enter is sent to our audit service, which checks that public site and returns a report to your browser. A copy of the report may be cached for up to ten minutes so a repeat check does not request the site again. We do not keep the report beyond that, and no account or email is needed. To prevent abuse, the service briefly counts recent audits from each IP address in memory; these counts are not saved.

Cookies, storage and analytics

This website does not set its own cookies. The scanner keeps your draft, and the audit keeps your latest report, in your browser’s session storage. It stays in that tab only and is cleared when you close it.

We may count page visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which Cloudflare says uses no cookies or local storage. It records the page visited, the referring page, browser type and page speed. It is not loaded on scanner report links, and it never receives your enquiry, scanner answers or audit reports.

Services that help run the site

  • Cloudflare hosts the website and runs the audit service. Like any web host, it processes technical details such as your IP address to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse.
  • Google Fonts supplies the site’s typeface, so your browser requests it from Google’s servers, which receive your IP address.
  • Web3Forms processes project enquiries, as described above.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the enquiry information we hold about you. Message us on WhatsApp (opens in a new tab) or send a project enquiry saying what you would like us to do, and we will reply using the details you give. We may need to confirm it is you before acting on a request.

We will update this page if the way we handle information changes.
