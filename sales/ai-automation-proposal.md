## 3. Proposed solution

An AI employee in a **front-desk role**, running on Staff and installed on [Client business name]'s website.

1. A customer opens the AI employee on the website and asks a question.
2. It answers from the services, prices, durations, opening hours and policies [Client business name] approved. If it does not know, it says so.
3. When the customer wants an appointment, it checks availability, reads the details back and books only after the customer says yes. If the booking is made, the customer gets a reference. If it cannot be confirmed, the customer is told the request has been received and the team will confirm.
4. Medical, clinical, complaint and unusual questions are handed to the team, with the customer's details, and the customer can reach the team on WhatsApp.
5. Enquiries and bookings appear on private owner screens.

**Booking option** (choose one in the assessment):

- **Option 1: Staff's built-in diary.** Online booking, rescheduling and cancellation happen in Staff. The team uses Staff's owner screens as the diary for online bookings.
- **Option 2: Keep the current booking method.** The AI employee takes booking requests and passes them to the team to confirm in the existing system. A direct connection to other booking software is integration work, quoted separately (section 6).

## 5. Implementation scope

The standard implementation covers **one location, one AI employee and one website**.

**Included**

1. Set-up of one AI employee in a front-desk role on Staff.
2. Business knowledge from information the client provides and approves: services, prices, durations, opening hours, location details, policies and common questions.
3. Booking set-up for the option chosen in section 3: services, durations and availability in Staff's diary, or booking requests to the team.
4. Enquiry capture, owner screens and handover to the team's WhatsApp number.
5. Rules for questions that must always go to a person, agreed with the client.
6. Installation on one website (WordPress, Wix, Webflow, Shopify or plain HTML), or a hosted Staff page link if the client has no website.
7. Testing with realistic conversations, including difficult ones.
8. One acceptance session and one team training session (up to 60 minutes each).
9. Two weeks of adjustments after launch.

- Integration with other booking software, CRMs, calendars or payment systems.

- Extra locations, AI employees, websites or languages.
- Writing new content (treatment descriptions, policies) beyond what the client provides.
- Changes to the client's website beyond installing the AI employee.

## 11. Security and data handling

- **Only what the job needs.** The AI employee collects a customer's name, contact details and the booking or enquiry details. It is configured not to ask for medical history, payment details or identity documents.
- **Private owner screens.** Owner screens are protected by private access codes and are never indexed by search engines. Access is limited to people the client names.
- **No payments.** Staff does not take payments or card details.
- **Logs without conversations.** Action logs record what was done and its outcome, not what the customer said.
- **Abuse protection.** Public pages use bot protection and rate and usage limits.
- **Retention.** Enquiry and booking records are kept for an agreed period ([e.g. 180 days for enquiries, 730 days for appointments]) and then deleted. The client can ask for records to be exported or deleted.
- **Transparency.** Customers are told they are talking to an AI assistant and can reach a person.
- **Client's obligations.** The client's own privacy notice should say that an AI assistant handles enquiries and bookings, and, if WhatsApp messages are in scope, that messages are sent with the customer's consent.
- **Credentials.** Passwords and API keys are never shared by email, WhatsApp or the enquiry form. Buildwise will agree a secure method for any access needed.

> Data protection law depends on the client's country (for example Nigeria's NDPA 2023, or UK or EU GDPR). Buildwise does not give legal advice; the client should confirm its own obligations.
