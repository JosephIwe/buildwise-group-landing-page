    How your data is handled
    • Your answers and notes are processed in this browser only. They are not sent to BuildWise Group or any other service.
    • A draft is kept in this browser tab so a refresh does not lose your progress. Closing the tab or starting a new scan clears it.
    • We may count page visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which Cloudflare says uses no cookies or local storage. It records the page visited, the referring page, browser type and page speed, never your answers, notes or report.
    • Report links contain your multiple-choice answers but not your notes or website, so a report opened from a link can rank slightly differently. Anyone you share a link with can see those answers.
    • If you add your website, the scanner does not visit or check it. It only appears in your report and in any message you choose to send.
    • “Request help” opens WhatsApp with a summary you can edit, including your answers, notes, website and a report link. Nothing is sent unless you send it.
