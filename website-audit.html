No sign-up. This is an automated technical audit, not a guarantee of search rankings, AI citations or customers.

How the audit works and what we keep
  • The address you enter is sent to BuildWise Group’s audit service, which runs on Cloudflare. It requests your page, robots.txt, a sitemap and up to five internal links, all as ordinary read-only page requests identified as BuildWiseAudit.
  • It never logs in, submits forms, runs your site’s scripts or changes anything. It only checks public websites, not private or internal addresses.
  • The report is shown in this browser. A copy may be cached for up to ten minutes so a repeat check does not request your site again; nothing else is stored, and no account or email is needed.
  • “Request help” opens WhatsApp with a summary you can edit. Nothing is sent unless you send it.
  • We may count page visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which records the page visited, never the address you audit or the report.

Enter a public page, usually your homepage, for example example.com.

  • Technical SEO Status, redirects, HTTPS, title, description, canonical, indexability, headings, sitemap and links.
  • Crawler access What your robots.txt allows search and AI crawlers to do, rule by rule.
