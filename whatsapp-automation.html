What WhatsApp automation is

From a busy inbox to a system that replies.

WhatsApp automation means software handles part of your WhatsApp conversations: greeting customers, answering common questions, collecting details, booking appointments and sending updates such as order confirmations. An AI chatbot adds a language model, so it can understand messages written in everyday language rather than only fixed menu choices.

The WhatsApp Business app on a phone offers simple greeting and away messages. Real automation runs on the WhatsApp Business Platform (the API), which Meta provides for businesses through its Cloud API or through partners. It supports multiple agents, integrations and automated replies, and it comes with rules: customers must opt in to receive messages from you, messages you start outside a customer-service window need pre-approved templates, and Meta charges for some message types.

We set this up properly, design the conversations, connect them to your systems and make sure a person can take over any chat at any time.

Frequently asked questions

Questions, answered plainly.

What is the difference between the WhatsApp Business app and the WhatsApp Business Platform?

The app is for small teams answering manually on a phone, with basic quick replies and away messages. The Platform (API) is for automation: chatbots, multiple agents, integrations and notifications at scale. It needs a Meta Business account and follows Meta’s policies.

How much does WhatsApp automation cost?

There are two parts: building the assistant, which we quote after discovery, and running costs. Running costs include Meta’s charges for certain message types, AI model usage and any hosting or partner fees. Meta sets and changes its own pricing, so we show you current rates for your country during scoping.

Can I keep my current WhatsApp number?

Often yes, but a number registered on the Platform can’t be used in the regular WhatsApp app at the same time. Many businesses use a new number for the automated line. We’ll walk through the options before anything changes.

Can it send messages to all my customers?

Only to customers who have opted in to hear from you, using approved message templates. Messaging people who haven’t opted in breaks WhatsApp’s policies and puts your number at risk.

Can it take payments?

It can send payment links from your payment provider and confirm when a payment comes through, if the provider supports that. Card details are never collected in the chat.

Does it understand Pidgin and local languages?

AI models handle Nigerian English and Pidgin reasonably well, and Yoruba, Igbo and Hausa less consistently. We test with real messages before launch and route anything uncertain to a person.
